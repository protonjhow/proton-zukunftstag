# Session 3: The lemonade company (who pays for free?)

The kids run a real lemonade business for the office:

1. They borrow CHF 10 from "the bank" (the finance team).
2. They shop at Migros and survey drink prices there.
3. They ask staff what they'd pay, then make and sell the lemonade.
4. They pay the bank back CHF 11 and split the profit.
5. Then: how could you give it away for free, and what would you have to take from people?

The full run sheet, shopping list, recipe and kit list are in
[`../day/facilitator-guide.md`](../day/facilitator-guide.md).

| File | What | Print (single-sided) |

Open each `.html` in Chrome and print at 100 % on A4. Colours are kept even
with "Background graphics" off.

idea also appears in `exec-brief.md`, `../day/facilitator-guide.md` and
